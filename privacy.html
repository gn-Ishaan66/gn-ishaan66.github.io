My Composio Poster - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

My Composio Poster ("the App") is a personal tool created and used by Ishaan to post and manage content on his own TikTok account.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by its developer, Ishaan, on his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. What the App accesses

When Ishaan logs in with TikTok, the App may access, through TikTok's official API and only with the permissions he approves: basic profile information, account statistics, a list of his own public videos, and the ability to upload and publish videos to his own account.

3. What is stored

The App does not build a database of personal information. Login tokens needed to keep the connection working are stored securely by the connection provider (Composio) so that the App can act on his behalf. No other personal data is collected or kept.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Data passes only between TikTok, the connection provider (Composio), and the developer's own tools.

5. Your choices

The TikTok connection can be revoked at any time from TikTok's account settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services) or by deleting the connection in Composio. Revoking access stops all further access by the App.
